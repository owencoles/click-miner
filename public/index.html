README.TXT

Real Bitcoin mining hardware performs on the order of hundreds of terahashes per second. You, clicking a button, perform roughly one hash every couple of seconds. The odds of you finding a block are not "small" — they're generation-ship-to-Andromeda small. That's the joke, and also the lesson: this is exactly what a miner does, just at a scale you can actually watch happen.
